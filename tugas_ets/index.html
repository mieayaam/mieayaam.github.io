About Us

ESQA Cosmetics is a clean beauty brand that takes pride in being vegan, ethical, and inclusive. We are here to celebrate every shade of you with our high-quality creations.

  • 100% Vegan & Cruelty-Free products.
  • Halal and BPOM certified for your peace of mind.
  • Formulated with clean and safe ingredients.

We believe that beauty should not compromise health, ethics, or inclusivity. Our cosmetics are designed to match the diverse skin tones of our customers while ensuring a smooth, luxurious texture and an effortless glow. Discover the real you with ESQA.
